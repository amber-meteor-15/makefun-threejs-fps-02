Foundry Clash is a round-based tactical first-person shooter for the browser, built on three.js: two teams of four fight over the Kestrel Foundry, an original dusk-lit yard with two plant sites, a mid block, and west and east lanes. The Wardens (your team) defend the sites; the Jackals attack with the Charge — a bomb they plant and you defuse. Between rounds an economy and buy menu let you spend earnings on a carbine, scattergun or scoped longbow, armor and grenades, while bot teammates and opponents navigate waypoint routes, hold angles, rotate on contact, trade burst fire where accuracy beats speed, and play the objective. First to nine rounds wins the match; everything runs offline from static files, with synthesized sound and no build step.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
# then open http://localhost:8080 in desktop Chrome
```
